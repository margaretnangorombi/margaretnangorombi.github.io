PROJECT OVERVIEW

Research focus

A nationwide study interviewed 2,095 fish consumers and examined hotels, restaurants, cafés and trade channels. Findings connected adoption to availability, reliable supply, freshness, cold-chain management and quality consistency.

This public case study is an anonymised summary. Client-confidential information and unpublished datasets are not displayed.
