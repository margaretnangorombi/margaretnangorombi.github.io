01 / ABOUT ME

Research discipline meets practical evidence.

I am a Research Analyst and Monitoring & Evaluation professional with over five years of experience supporting research, evaluation, data collection, analysis, reporting and evidence-based decision-making.

My experience spans development, financial services, energy, governance, health and consumer markets. I have supported assignments from planning and questionnaire development through digital survey programming, fieldwork coordination, data-quality checks, analysis and presentation of findings.

I work across quantitative and qualitative methods, including national surveys, impact evaluations, public-opinion research, focus group discussions, key informant interviews, CATI studies and stakeholder consultations.

My approach combines sound research practice, reliable data management and clear communication so that findings are useful to the people making decisions.
