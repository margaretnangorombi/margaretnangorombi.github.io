Financial Inclusion Case Study 01

Small Business Finance Is Helping Women Grow — But Access Still Has Friction

Women entrepreneurs described using financing to restock, expand product ranges, improve equipment and strengthen business operations.

Training and mentorship also supported better budgeting, savings discipline and clearer separation of household and business money. At the same time, participants raised practical barriers including slow loan processing, financing amounts that did not always match business needs, collateral concerns and the time required to attend training.

MY ROLE

Research support • Data analysis • Interpretation • Report writing

EVIDENCE PRODUCED

Insights on business growth, access to finance, financial management and programme improvement.

Market Research Case Study 02

A Brand Can Be Highly Visible Without Truly Connecting With Consumers

The study looked beyond simple visibility to understand how consumers actually experienced sponsorships, brand activity and customer engagement.

The research examined whether consumers merely noticed brand activity or whether those encounters influenced awareness, interaction and overall perception. The findings were translated into evidence that could guide future brand, sponsorship and engagement decisions.

MY ROLE

Consumer research • Data analysis • Interpretation • Reporting

EVIDENCE PRODUCED

Findings on visibility, sponsorship impact and consumer engagement.

The evaluation examined whether a major development campaign was reaching its intended audiences and whether its messages were remembered and acted upon.

Research explored awareness, recall, message penetration and behaviour following exposure, helping distinguish between simply broadcasting communications and creating meaningful audience response.

Consumer Product Testing Case Study 07

When Consumers Taste a Product Without Seeing the Brand, Preferences Become Clearer

A blind consumer test compared four seasoning products without allowing branding or packaging to influence the initial judgement.

Thirty consumers completed the product test and 11 joined a follow-up group discussion. Participants assessed appearance, taste, flavour, aroma, texture, firmness and purchase appeal, revealing clear differences in overall liking between the samples.

MY ROLE

Questionnaire design • Recruitment • Team training • Product-testing execution • Report writing

EVIDENCE PRODUCED

Comparative evidence on sensory performance, consumer preference and purchase appeal.

Education & Nutrition Case Study 08

Schools Already Know About Whole Grains — The Challenge Is Turning Awareness Into Everyday Habits

A baseline involving 698 respondents across 19 schools examined learners, parents, cooks and administrators to understand readiness for greater use of fortified whole-grain foods.

Awareness was already high, shifting the programme challenge away from simple exposure and toward deeper knowledge, positive attitudes, trial and sustained adoption within school feeding systems.

MY ROLE

Report writing • Insight generation • Interpretation of findings

EVIDENCE PRODUCED

Behavioural insights showing where future school-feeding interventions should focus.

Youth & Consumer Trends Case Study 09

Kenya's Children Are Growing Up Digital — But Their Experiences Are Far From Uniform

Research comparing children and parents found that digital access is still heavily tied to shared household technology.

Most children regularly used a parent or caregiver's smartphone, while personal smartphone ownership increased with age. Regional differences were also often stronger than gender differences, creating very different digital experiences across the country.

MY ROLE

Report writing • Insight generation • Interpretation of quantitative and qualitative evidence

EVIDENCE PRODUCED

Decision-ready insights on children's digital behaviour, household influence and future consumer trends.

Food Market Research Case Study 10

Consumers Know the Product — So Why Isn't Consumption Higher?

A nationwide study interviewed 2,095 fish consumers and also examined the market through hotels, restaurants, cafés and trade channels.

The evidence showed that market growth required more than consumer awareness. Availability, product presentation, reliable supply, freshness, cold-chain management and quality consistency all affected adoption.

MY ROLE

Questionnaire design • Recruitment • Team training • Project management • Report writing

EVIDENCE PRODUCED

A market-wide view connecting consumer demand with supply-chain and distribution realities.

Beauty & Personal Care Case Study 11

What Women Buy in Beauty Depends on More Than Price

A quantitative study interviewed 362 women aged 18–45 across urban and rural locations to understand cosmetics use, spending and purchasing behaviour.

The research explored commonly used products and brands, expenditure, acceptable pricing, preferred pack sizes, willingness to try new brands, distribution channels and unmet needs. Body-care products had the broadest usage, while the wider evidence highlighted the combined role of routines, performance, affordability and availability.

MY ROLE

Questionnaire design • Recruitment • Team training • Project management • Report writing

EVIDENCE PRODUCED

Consumer insights supporting product development, pricing, packaging, distribution and market-entry decisions.
