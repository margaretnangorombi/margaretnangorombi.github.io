Financial Inclusion • Impact Evaluation 01

Women-Led Enterprise Impact Evaluation

A mixed-method evaluation examining the experiences, business outcomes and financing realities of women-led enterprises participating in a financial inclusion programme.

WORK DONE

Supported research delivery, qualitative and quantitative evidence synthesis, data analysis and reporting.

MILESTONE / VALUE

Converted participant experiences and programme findings into decision-ready evidence on access, business growth, barriers and areas for programme improvement.

CASE STUDY

Women entrepreneurs described using finance to restock, expand product ranges, improve equipment and grow sales. Training and mentorship were also linked to stronger budgeting, savings discipline, customer relations and better separation of business and household money. At the same time, participants raised practical barriers including slow loan processing, insufficient amounts, collateral concerns and the time or travel cost of attending training.
